### CHEM-000457: Water’s catalytic step in zinc-soap crystallization
- **Subfield:** Round 6
- **Open:** Identify the coordination-changing step through which trace water accelerates zinc-carboxylate crystallization in cured oil. Measure the relevant intermediate, water dependence, and possible catalytic turnover.
- **Status:** In 2023, swelling alone did not cause the acceleration, while water-containing solvent induced crystallization. Stabilization of transient charged states was proposed without direct observation of the intermediate.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Explains why small water impurities in treatment solvents reorganize metal soaps.
- **Key works:** J. Hermans, K. Helwig, S. Woutersen, and K. Keune (2023), “Traces of water catalyze zinc soap crystallization in solvent-exposed oil paints,” [DOI: 10.1039/D2CP04861B](https://doi.org/10.1039/D2CP04861B).
